### 1. Our Story

Founded in 2001, Media Model is a research and advisory collective helping organizations navigate complexity through evidence, analysis, and strategic insight.

From the very beginning, we set out to understand Armenia - its people, markets, institutions, and evolving realities. Over the years, this deep local knowledge has made us a trusted partner for complex decisions.

For more than two decades, we have worked with public institutions, private organizations, political stakeholders, and international development agencies—transforming data into direction and research into action.

As our work evolved, so did our scope. Since 2012, Media Model has expanded into land acquisition, resettlement, and social development consulting, leading some of Armenia's largest and most complex projects within national infrastructure and development programs.

Today, we operate as a research collective, bringing together the right expertise for each challenge. Our network includes sociologists, economists, statisticians, political scientists, legal experts, and field specialists - allowing us to respond to complex problems with clarity and precision.

At the intersection of research, advisory, and implementation, we help organizations model the way from complexity to clarity.

We know Armenia. We study it, measure it, and help it move forward.

### Research Services

We help organizations understand people, systems, and change—turning data into insight and insight into action.

**Social & Public Opinion Research**
We design and implement qualitative and quantitative studies to understand public attitudes, behaviors, and societal trends.

**Market & Consumer Research**
We provide deep insights into customer behavior, market dynamics, and brand perception to support strategic decision-making.

**Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E)**
We support organizations in tracking performance, measuring impact, and improving programs through structured evaluation frameworks.

**Policy Analysis & Advisory**
We combine research and expert analysis to support policy development, strategic planning, and evidence-based decision-making.

### Land Acquisition & Resettlement (LARP) Services

We provide full-cycle implementation of land acquisition and resettlement processes in line with national legislation and international standards.

**Full Implementation of LARP Processes**
We organize and implement the complete process of land acquisition for properties recognized as of public interest, in compliance with the legislation of the Republic of Armenia and relevant international policies.

**Key Areas of Work**
- Preparation of acquisition contracts and compensation agreements
- Notification and communication processes
- Negotiation and support for affected persons
- Legal and documentation support, including inheritance and ownership issues
- Preparation and coordination of cadastral and notarial transactions
- Organization of state registration of acquired property
- Initiation and management of court procedures for property acquisition

All processes are carried out in close cooperation with relevant state authorities and local community bodies.
